## Tool trolleys: your questions, answered.

What is the difference between the Max Rescue case and the Max Titan Minibox?

The Max Rescue service case (BRU-MDP, €199 incl. VAT) is a light 13 kg trolley on wheels with 110 tools and over 300 accessories. The Max Titan Minibox (BRU-MBX, €350 incl. VAT) is a 22 kg three-drawer metal box with 83 chrome-vanadium steel tools and central locking.
