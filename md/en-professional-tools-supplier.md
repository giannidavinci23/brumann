# Professional tools supplier for distributors

Our ambition: to support our distributors in acquiring and developing a complete range of tools. Trolleys, workshop furniture, service cases and tools under one brand. Trade prices on request.

[Image : Brumann Max Edition workshop tool trolley]

## A complete range under one brand

Four product families, which you can list together or separately.

- [Max Edition trolley](en-max-edition-tool-trolley): 261 tools, 8 drawers, two torque wrenches.
- [Max Titan Minibox](en-3-drawer-minibox): metal tool chest, 83 tools, 3 drawers.
- [Max Rescue case](en-service-tool-case): 110 tools and over 300 accessories, on wheels.
- [Max Workshop furniture](en-workshop-furniture): bench, cabinets and panels, supplied without tools.

## Why list Brumann

- A complete range: trolleys, furniture, cases and tools under one brand.
- A range that evolves: products follow the market and our distributors’ needs.
- In your colours: branding for your store, on request.
- A direct contact: a sales director reachable by phone and e-mail.

## An offer in your colours

We study branding for your store: see [white-label custom tools](en-custom-branded-tools-distributors).

## How to get started

- Download the 26/27 catalogue and pick the products you are interested in.
- Send a distributor request: site form, phone or contact[at]brumann.fr.
