# About Brumann

A French brand of tool trolleys, workshop furniture and professional tools, born from two trades where close enough is never enough: motorsport and aviation.

## Our story

One comes from motorsport, the other from aviation. In 2021 they started an import-export business to meet requests from the people around them: quality products and a personal service.

Unable to find a manufacturer that met their standards, they designed their own tool trolley, with built-in torque wrenches and professional-grade tools, at a sensible price. The Brumann brand was born.

Brumann is a company in its own right, part of a group that includes an aviation branch and a flight school. The range keeps evolving with the needs of distributors and users.

## Milestones

- 2021 · Import-export : Selected products for the founders’ circle.
- 2021 · The first trolley : Designed for motorsport and aviation, with built-in torque wrenches.
- 24/25 · Growing the distributor network : Structuring and expanding the network of partner resellers.
- 26/27 · A complete range : Trolleys, workshop furniture, cases and custom branding for distributors.
