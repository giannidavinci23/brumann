## Which solution should you choose?

- To equip a complete workshop: the [Max Edition trolley](en-max-edition-tool-trolley), 261 chrome-vanadium steel tools, eight drawers and two torque wrenches (5–25 and 40–210 N·m).
- For a more compact station: the [Max Titan Minibox](en-3-drawer-minibox), 83 tools, three drawers, 22 kg.
- To work on site: the [Max Rescue service case](en-service-tool-case), 110 tools and more than 300 accessories, on wheels, 13 kg.
- To fit out the workshop itself: [Max Workshop furniture](en-workshop-furniture), configured to order, supplied without tools.
