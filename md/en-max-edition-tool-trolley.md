## Tool trolleys: your questions, answered.

What does the Max Edition tool trolley contain?

The Max Edition trolley (ref. BRU-SER-8T) holds 261 chrome vanadium tools in 8 drawers: six fitted drawers (sockets, bits, torque wrenches, screwdrivers, pliers, T-handle keys), two free drawers, an aluminium top shelf and a side storage cabinet. It measures 84 × 98 × 46 cm, weighs 85 kg and retails at €899 including VAT.
Which torque wrenches are included?

The Max Edition comes with two torque wrenches: a ¼″ wrench from 5 to 25 N·m for precision work, and a ½″ wrench from 40 to 210 N·m for everyday workshop tightening.
Which finishes are available?

The Max Edition comes in Nardo grey, black and red, or black. Lacquered metallic paint, reinforced runners, central key locking, self-locking drawers and quiet rubber castors.
