Our storySince 2021

Two founders. Two worlds where close enough is never enough.

LFPNICAO code of Toussus-le-Noble aerodrome, home of the brand.

One comes from motorsport, the other from aviation. In 2021 they started an import-export business to meet requests from the people around them: quality products and a personal service.

Unable to find a manufacturer that met their standards, they designed their own tool trolley, with built-in torque wrenches and professional-grade tools, at a sensible price. The Brumann brand was born.

Today Brumann belongs to a flight school based at the aerodrome. The range keeps evolving with the needs of distributors and users.

Your questions, answered.

Range, prices, finishes and distribution: the essentials about Brumann.

What is Brumann?

Brumann is a French brand of tool trolleys, workshop furniture and professional tools, designed for motorsport and aviation. It is based at Toussus-le-Noble aerodrome (LFPN), south-west of Paris, and belongs to a flight school on the airfield.

What does the Max Edition tool trolley contain?

The Max Edition trolley (ref. BRU-SER-8T) holds 261 chrome vanadium tools in 8 drawers: six fitted drawers (sockets, bits, torque wrenches, screwdrivers, pliers, T-handle keys), two free drawers, an aluminium top shelf and a side storage cabinet. It measures 84 × 98 × 46 cm, weighs 85 kg and retails at €899 including VAT.

Which torque wrenches are included?

The Max Edition comes with two torque wrenches: a ¼″ wrench from 5 to 25 N·m for precision work, and a ½″ wrench from 40 to 210 N·m for everyday workshop tightening.

Which finishes are available?

The Max Edition comes in Nardo grey, black and red, or black. Lacquered metallic paint, reinforced runners, central key locking, self-locking drawers and quiet rubber castors.

What is the difference between the Max Rescue case and the Max Titan Minibox?

The Max Rescue service case (BRU-MDP, €199 incl. VAT) is a light 13 kg trolley on wheels with 110 tools and over 300 accessories. The Max Titan Minibox (BRU-MBX, €350 incl. VAT) is a 22 kg three-drawer metal box with 83 certified chrome vanadium tools and central locking.

How much does the workshop furniture cost?

Max Workshop furniture (cabinets, trolleys and worktop) is configured to fit your space, from €1,250 including VAT. 0.6 mm steel, 28 mm wooden worktop, up to 260 cm wide and 190 cm high, 138 to 165 kg depending on version, delivered in 1 or 2 packages and supplied without tools. Colours adaptable to the volume.

Can products be made in our brand colours?

Yes, on request: spanner sets, tool rolls, toolboxes, tool sets and drawer modules can be produced in a distributor’s colours. Drawers 7 and 8 of the Max Edition can also be fitted to order.
